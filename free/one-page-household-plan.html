HOW TO FILL IN YOUR ONE PAGE

This page is your household budget for one month. It has four boxes. Fill them in order, from Box 1 to Box 4. Make a new page for each month. A pen is all you need.

Box 1: What Comes In

Write the date of every payday this month. Next to each date, write the amount you expect.

If your checks are not always the same, write the smallest check you normally get.

If two adults bring money into the house, write both paychecks here. It is one house, so it is one page.

Box 2: What Must Go Out

This box is in order. The order tells you what gets paid first when money is tight.

Lines 1 to 5 are giving, food, lights and water, the roof over your head, and the ride to work. Write the amount for each one. These get paid before anything else.

Under Other Bills, write every other bill you pay each month, the day it is due, and the amount.

Box 3: Payday by Payday

Write one payday on each Payday line, in order.

Look at the due date of each bill in Box 2. Find the last payday before that date. Write the bill on the Bills line under that payday.

Write how much of that paycheck goes to food and to gas.

If one payday has too many bills under it, move a bill to the payday before it. You can also call the company and ask for a different due date.

When every bill under a payday is paid, check the Paid box.

Box 4: What's Left

What is left after the bills gets three jobs.

Job 1 is savings. Write an amount to save every payday, even five dollars.

Job 2 is the things that come once a year, like car tags, school clothes and Christmas. Write each one and a small amount to set aside for it.

Job 3 is spending money. Write the amount, take it out in cash, and put it in an envelope. When the envelope is empty, spending is done until the next payday.

In a tight week, the once-a-year things wait first. Savings and lines 1 to 5 in Box 2 do not wait.

Check-in day

Pick one day of the week and write it at the bottom of the page.

On that day, sit down with the page for five minutes. Check off the bills that got paid, and check the box for that week.

If a paycheck was smaller than you planned, write which bill has to move to the next payday.

If another adult in your house spends the money too, do the check-in together.

Next month

In the last week of the month, take a clean page and fill it in for the next month. Most of it copies straight over.
