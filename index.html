The Five Layer System

I built this system for my own kitchen before I ever made a video. With it, dinner is no longer a decision you make at 5 PM. Here is how it works.

  1. VisibilityKnow what you have.Before you plan or shop, look at your freezer, fridge and pantry. You cannot use what you cannot see.
  2. BasesCook your proteins ahead.One day a week, cook your meat plain, bag it and freeze it. One bag is one family dinner.
  3. SupportsAdd what makes it a meal.Vegetables, rice, beans and sauces. Keep them frozen, canned or dry, ready to use.
  4. Emergency MealsCover the hard nights.Keep six fully cooked meals in your freezer. Six covers one whole week.
  5. MaintenanceRestock once a week.One cooking session replaces what you used, so you start every week full.

On a normal night you pull a bag of protein, add your supports, season it and eat. Meal Builder Pro and the Kitchen inside Household COO do these five steps for you. You can also do every step with a notebook and a pen, and I show you how on YouTube.

The Provisioned Household

Understand your Bible for yourself.

The Provisioned Household is for anyone who wants to build a relationship with Christ. In each study I take one passage and one problem people face today, like worry, anger, guilt or feeling alone, and we find out what the Bible says about it.

New studies come out every Sunday, Wednesday and Friday. Over time, you learn to open any passage and study it on your own.
